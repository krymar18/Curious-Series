Find What a Document Does Not Say

Ask specific questions of a document, then surface what it does not cover — the gaps are often more important than the content itself.

8 – 10 min Any long document, policy, or contract M365 Copilot Premium
1
Ask a specific question first
Copilot Chat
▶
Do this
Open Copilot Chat with a long document (upload it or reference it via SharePoint). Start with a targeted question rather than a general summary request.
Prompt 1 — Specific question

Based on this document, what is the process for [specific task or scenario]? List the steps in order. If the document does not cover it, say so explicitly rather than inferring from related content.

2
Surface what is missing
Copilot Chat
▶
Do this
In the same thread, run the gaps prompt:
Prompt 2 — Find the gaps

What important topics or scenarios does this document not address at all? What questions would a reader have after reading it that the document does not answer?

3
Surface the unstated assumptions
Copilot Chat
▶
Do this
Pick one gap that matters for your work and ask:
Prompt 3 — Surface the assumption

What assumption does this document make about [the gap topic] that it never states explicitly?

◈
Reflect
Gaps and unstated assumptions are where disputes originate. If you are relying on this document to govern a process or decision, what you found here needs to be resolved before you rely on it.
Key Takeaways
  • AI answers specific questions from a document reliably. Identifying the right question to ask is still your job — and it is the harder half.
  • The gaps prompt is one of the highest-value document analysis prompts. Most analysis focuses on what is there; gaps are where things go wrong.
  • Assumptions a document makes but never states are the source of most downstream disputes. Surface them now, not after you have relied on them.
