Simulate a Difficult Stakeholder

Describe a specific stakeholder and ask Copilot to respond to your proposal as they would — rigorously, not diplomatically — then ask what would actually convince them.

8 – 10 min A real proposal or plan, Copilot Chat M365 Copilot Premium
1
Describe the stakeholder
You
▶
Do this
Think of one stakeholder you regularly need to persuade. Write 2–3 sentences describing: their role, their stated priorities, and one or two things they typically push back on. The more specific, the more useful the simulation.
2
Simulate their response
Copilot Chat
▶
Do this
Open Copilot Chat. Fill in the persona description from your notes above:
Prompt 1 — Simulate

Read this proposal and respond as [describe the persona — role, priorities, typical objections]. Find the real weaknesses. Be rigorous, not diplomatic — I need the pushback I will actually face in the room, not a polished version of it.

◉
Look for
Is the pushback substantive or generic? If every objection could apply to any proposal, the persona description was not specific enough. Add more detail about this person's actual priorities and run it again.
3
Ask what would actually convince them
Copilot Chat
▶
Do this
In the same thread:
Prompt 2 — What would convince them?

What would actually convince this person to say yes to this proposal? Not what sounds persuasive in general — what specifically addresses what they care most about, based on what you described about their priorities?

◈
Reflect
Which objections are valid? Which are weak? For the valid ones — does your proposal need to change, or does your pitch?
Key Takeaways
  • Role-level personas give directional insight; person-level descriptions with priorities and objection history give useful simulation.
  • Copilot defaults to diplomatic pushback. Tell it explicitly to be rigorous — "not a polished version of it" is the instruction that produces the useful output.
  • A compelling AI counterargument is not necessarily a valid one. Evaluate every objection on its merits independently.
