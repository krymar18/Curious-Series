Lab A1 · Teams

Summarise a Meeting

Produce a structured plain-language summary of a real meeting — decisions, information shared, and what is still unresolved — without reading any notes first.

8 – 10 min A recorded Teams meeting with transcript M365 Copilot Premium
1
Open the meeting in Teams
Teams · Copilot
▶
Do this
Open the meeting in Teams. Click the Recap tab, then open Copilot. Stay in the same thread throughout this challenge — do not open a new chat.
Copy prompt — Summary

Summarise this meeting in plain language. Structure it as: (1) Purpose — what was the meeting for? (2) Key decisions made. (3) Key information shared. (4) What was not resolved or left open. Keep each section to 3 sentences maximum.

◉
Look for
Is the "not resolved" section accurate, or is it empty when it should not be? That section is the most important part of any meeting summary — and the most commonly wrong.
◈
Reflect
Did Copilot miss anything you consider significant? What type of thing — a decision, a concern, a commitment? That gap tells you where you still need to be the note-taker.
2
Ask Copilot what it missed
Teams · Copilot
▶
Do this
In the same thread, send this follow-up before closing:
Follow-up — Confidence check

What are you least confident about in this summary? Where might the transcript have been unclear or incomplete? Tell me where I should check against the original recording.

◉
Look for
Does Copilot identify real gaps — moments where multiple people spoke at once, technical terms that may have been transcribed incorrectly, or tone that was ambiguous? This self-critique prompt is one of the most useful habits you can build.
Key Takeaways
  • The "not resolved" section is the most important part of a meeting summary. If Copilot leaves it empty, it is probably wrong — ask for it explicitly.
  • Copilot summarises based on transcript patterns, not on what mattered to you. Missing content is not an error — it is a gap you need to fill.
  • Asking Copilot what it is least confident about is how you find out where to check the recording. Build it into every meeting summary.
