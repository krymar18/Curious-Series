Lab A2 · Teams

Extract and Verify Action Items

Pull every action item from a meeting with owners, deadlines, and whether each was agreed or assigned — then verify at least one attribution against the transcript.

8 – 10 min A recorded Teams meeting (or output from A1) M365 Copilot Premium
1
Extract all action items
Teams · Copilot
▶
Do this
Open Copilot in your Teams meeting recap. If you ran A1, continue in the same thread — Copilot already has context. Paste the prompt below.
Copy prompt

List every action item from this meeting. For each one: (1) the action, (2) who owns it by name, (3) any deadline mentioned, (4) whether the person explicitly agreed to it or it was assigned to them. If no deadline was mentioned, say so.

◉
Look for
Check whether actions are attributed to the right people. Misattribution is the most common error in AI meeting notes — the action exists but the owner is wrong.
2
Verify at least one attribution
Teams · Transcript
▶
Do this
Pick one action item. Find the moment in the transcript where it was discussed. Read the original exchange. Does the attribution match what was actually said?
◈
Reflect
Was the attribution correct? If not, what type of error was it — wrong person, wrong action, or a commitment that was implied rather than stated? That error type tells you where to focus your spot-checking next time.
⚑ Ethics

Before sharing AI-generated action items with the people they name: read every item first. An incorrect attribution creates a written record with wrong information. You are responsible for what you forward — AI-generated does not mean reviewed.

Key Takeaways
  • Misattribution is the most common error in AI meeting notes — not fabrication. The action is real; the owner may be wrong.
  • The difference between "explicitly agreed" and "assigned" matters for follow-up conversations. Copilot flags this distinction, but only if you ask.
  • You are responsible for what you forward. Read every attributed item before it reaches the person it names.
